## Customer Service

The contact-center and field-service view: resolve the customer in front of you
faster, and reach out before problems become calls.

### Use cases

- **Whole-customer lookup** — search to a single profile spanning every account,
  premise, and meter, with current billing, usage, and service status.
- **Active-outage awareness** — see immediately whether a caller's premise is in
  a known outage, so agents stop guessing.
- **Proactive complaint outreach** — the complaint-risk model scores each
  customer's likelihood of an imminent complaint, so retention effort lands
  before dissatisfaction does.

### Business value

Shorter handle times and first-contact resolution; fewer repeat calls; retained
customers who would otherwise have churned quietly.

### Powered by

`dim_customer` and the account/premise hierarchy ([the data
model](doc:data-model)), the complaint-risk `ml_*` table, and the CSAT / FCR
metric views. Demonstrated today in the **CSAT** view and the customer profile
drawer.
