## Revenue & Collections

The credit-and-collections view: protect revenue while steering at-risk
customers toward assistance rather than disconnection.

### Use cases

- **Payment-stress cohorts** — segment customers by arrears, payment history,
  and billing trend to see who is falling behind and how fast.
- **Assistance targeting** — identify likely LIHEAP-eligible and hardship
  customers for proactive outreach before balances become uncollectible.
- **Collections prioritization** — focus recovery effort where balances and
  recovery odds are highest.

### Business value

Lower bad-debt write-off; more customers kept current through early
intervention; assistance dollars directed to the customers who most need them.

### Powered by

`fact_customer_billing` (charges, payments, arrears) joined to customer
attributes ([the data model](doc:data-model)), explored as cohorts on the
Explorer map's grain and lens controls.
